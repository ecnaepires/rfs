## 3. Model — "How the group works"

Three tiers, top to bottom (matches the current site layout):

**EN**
1. **RFS — Long-horizon governance.** Sets the group's direction, capital allocation, and portfolio strategy across every company.
2. **Sunergies Group — Integrated operating platform.** Coordinates commercial, financial, and operational execution across the ecosystem; runs shared services and cross-company synergy.
3. **Ecosystem Companies — Specialized teams, market by market.** Ten focused businesses, each led by its own team, operating with autonomy inside the group's model.

**PT**
1. **RFS — Governança de longo prazo.** Define a direção, a alocação de capital e a estratégia de portfólio de todas as empresas.
2. **Grupo Sunergies — Plataforma operacional integrada.** Coordena a execução comercial, financeira e operacional do ecossistema; opera serviços compartilhados e sinergia entre empresas.
3. **Empresas do ecossistema — Equipes especializadas, mercado a mercado.** Dez negócios focados, cada um liderado por sua própria equipe, operando com autonomia dentro do modelo do grupo.

*Image: the gold-on-black Sunergies hub-and-spoke diagram (Image 1) is the natural fit — it visually is this three-tier model in one graphic.*
