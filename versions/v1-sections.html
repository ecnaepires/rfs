RFS is the holding company behind Sunergies Group and its ecosystem — a single governance structure over independent businesses spanning generation, government and corporate energy deals, alternative mobility, digital energy assets, media and R&D. Each company operates with its own team and market focus; RFS sets the direction, capital discipline and time horizon.
