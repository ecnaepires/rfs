# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Institutional audiences evaluating the group behind Sunergies: family offices and investment
funds (domestic and international), potential partners for the business units, public-sector
counterparts who want to know who stands behind a bid, and senior hires. They arrive from a
pitch deck, a referral, or due diligence, mostly on desktop. The job: conclude in one visit
that RFS is a serious, long-horizon holding company — patrimonial, structured, permanent —
not a startup umbrella.

## Product Purpose

Corporate site for RFS Grupo Holding (Brasil), the holding company above Sunergies Group and
its ten business units. Bilingual: **English is the default language** (international
investors), Portuguese one click away. Success = a visitor understands the three-tier
structure (RFS → Sunergies Group → units), feels the scale and permanence, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Patrimonial gold on black. A holding company's voice: fewer words, larger type, longer
horizon. The confidence of an annual report crossed with the energy of a sunrise — the brand
mark IS a gold sun over a horizon ("Horizonte Protegido"). Alive: the page breathes with
deliberate, engineered motion; nothing static, nothing frantic.

Identity is fixed by the owner's identity studies: **gold #D4AF37 + black #000000**, vector
assets only. Typographic kinship with Sunergies (Archivo variable) — the holding speaks in
the expanded width, the operating brand in the normal width.

## Anti-references

- Generic "luxury" gold sites: script fonts, marble textures, gradient-gold everything.
- Crypto/web3 landing grammar (glow orbs, glassmorphism), even though Sundex touches tokens.
- SaaS scaffolding: cream backgrounds, eyebrow labels on every section, identical card grids.
- The first ecosystem mockup's static schematic — RFS must feel alive, not archival.

## Design Principles

1. Black is the room, gold is the light — gold is applied like light hitting metal: edges,
   lines, one drenched band; never gold body text over large areas.
2. Holding-company cadence — one dominant idea per viewport; big type, short sentences.
3. Motion is the brand's pulse — an orchestrated entrance, then a slow persistent breathing
   (shimmer, pulsing map points); every animation has a reduced-motion fallback.
4. Never invent facts — RFS has no published history/numbers; only Sunergies' verified facts
   (2014, 500+ MW, end-to-end delivery) may be stated. Contact details are owner-supplied
   placeholders until confirmed.
5. Both languages are first-class — PT copy is written, not machine-flavored; the toggle is
   instant and remembered.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA on black: body text ≥4.5:1 (gold #D4AF37 on near-black passes; verify every muted
tone), black text on the gold drenched band, visible focus rings in gold, semantic landmarks,
`lang` attribute switches with the language toggle, full `prefers-reduced-motion` coverage.
